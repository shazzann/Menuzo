# Menuzo — Problem Analysis & Solution Report

## Traditional Restaurant Menus vs Digital Menu Platform

## 1. Executive Summary

Restaurants still rely heavily on printed menus to present their food offerings. While printed menus are simple, they create several operational problems: limited availability during busy hours, outdated information, expensive updates, poor customer experience, and zero marketing opportunities.

**Menuzo transforms the traditional menu into a smart digital experience.**
Instead of static paper, restaurants get a dynamic QR-based menu that customers can access instantly, while owners can manage, update, customize, and promote their menu from a single platform.

---

# 2. Problems with Traditional Restaurant Menus

## Problem 1: Limited Menu Availability During Busy Hours

### Current Situation

Many restaurants operate with fewer physical menus than tables.

Example:

* Restaurant has **20 tables**
* Available menus: **5–10 copies**
* During peak hours:

  * Multiple tables need menus at the same time
  * Waiters must collect and redistribute menus
  * Customers have to wait before ordering

### Business Impact

* Slower table turnover
* Increased workload for waiters
* Delayed ordering process
* Poor first impression for customers
* Less efficient service during crowded periods

### Menuzo Solution

### QR-Based Instant Menu Access

Customers can:

1. Sit at the table
2. Scan the QR code
3. Open the complete menu instantly

Benefits:

* Every table has access to the menu simultaneously
* No waiting for physical menus
* Faster ordering decisions
* Better customer experience

---

# Problem 2: Poor Customer Experience When Sharing a Physical Menu

## Current Situation

A single menu creates a difficult experience for groups.

Example:

A table with 6 people receives one menu.

Customers have to:

* Pass the menu around
* Lean toward each other
* Wait for others to finish reading
* Discuss limited information from one screen/page

### Business Impact

* Less comfortable dining experience
* Slower decision-making
* Customers may skip exploring additional dishes

### Menuzo Solution

## Individual Digital Menu Access

Every customer can open the menu on their own smartphone.

Benefits:

* Everyone can browse freely
* Multiple people can view different categories
* Easier group discussion
* Customers spend more time exploring dishes

# Problem 3: Customers Select Unavailable Items

## Current Situation

Printed menus cannot reflect real-time availability.

Example:

Customer chooses:

> "Special Chicken Burger"

After waiting:

> "Sorry, it is unavailable."

Customer must:

* Choose again
* Review the menu again
* Spend additional time deciding

### Business Impact

* Customer frustration
* Slower ordering
* Negative dining experience

### Menuzo Solution

## Real-Time Menu Management

Restaurant owners can:

* Mark items as unavailable
* Hide temporary unavailable items
* Update menu information instantly

Benefits:

* Customers see accurate information
* Reduces disappointment
* Improves ordering efficiency
