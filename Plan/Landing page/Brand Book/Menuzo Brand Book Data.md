
A proper brand book is not just a logo and color palette. It should become the single source of truth for everyone who designs, writes, markets, or builds Menuzo.

Since Menuzo is a SaaS product, your brand book should cover strategy first, visuals second.

## Recommended Length

For Menuzo, aim for a **60–100 page brand book**, organized into these major sections:

| Section                 | Pages |
| ----------------------- | ----: |
| Brand Strategy          | 10–15 |
| Visual Identity         | 20–25 |
| Product Design System   | 15–20 |
| Marketing Guidelines    | 10–15 |
| Applications & Examples | 15–25 |

This structure gives your designers, developers, marketers, and future team members a complete reference. As Menuzo grows, it can evolve into a combined **Brand Book + Design System + Marketing Playbook**, ensuring consistency across the product, website, and every customer touchpoint.
