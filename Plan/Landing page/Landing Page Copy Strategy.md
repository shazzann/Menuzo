## One Critical Recommendation

Avoid making the landing page about **features**. Restaurant owners don't buy "QR codes," "themes," or "analytics." They buy outcomes.

Frame every section around business value:

* ❌ Generate QR Codes → ✅ Replace printed menus permanently.
* ❌ Theme Customizer → ✅ Make your restaurant look premium.
* ❌ Analytics → ✅ Discover your best-selling dishes.
* ❌ Dashboard → ✅ Update your menu in seconds instead of reprinting.

This shift from feature-first to benefit-first messaging will significantly improve conversion.
