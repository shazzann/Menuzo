A badge system is a good idea, but keep it **small, meaningful, and scannable**. If every item has 8–10 badges, they lose value.

I'd split them into **Global Badges** (restaurant-wide) and **Food Badges** (specific to a dish).

---

# ⭐ Recommended Core Set for Menuzo

If you want a clean and consistent system, I'd start with these:

### Global Badges

* Halal
* Vegetarian Friendly
* Vegan Friendly
* Dine-In
* Takeaway
* Delivery
* Freshly Made
* Card Accepted

### Food Badges

* Best Seller
* Popular
* New
* Chef's Special
* Limited Time
* Mild
* Medium Spicy
* Hot
* Extra Hot
* Vegetarian
* Vegan
* Gluten Free
* High Protein
* Healthy Choice
* Combo
* Family Size
* Breakfast
* Lunch
* Dinner
* Freshly Cooked

This gives restaurants enough flexibility while keeping the badge system understandable and visually uncluttered. I would also recommend limiting each food item to **a maximum of 3–4 badges** on the menu card, with the full list visible on the food detail page.
